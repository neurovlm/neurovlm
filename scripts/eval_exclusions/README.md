# Evaluation exclusions (`scripts/eval_exclusions/`)

The single source of truth for the items that every NeuroVLM evaluation drops because they leak the training data.
Both files are generated by the leakage audit (reviewer comment 2.4), never edited by hand.

| file | rows | what is excluded |
|---|---|---|
| `pubmed_exclusions.csv` | 42 = **16 test-set + 26 val-set** publications (one row per PMID) | held-out PubMed publications with a *strict* near-duplicate across splits: in the training split (38), in the other held-out split (3, test↔val coordinate tables) or in the NeuroVault set (1) |
| `neurovault_exclusions.csv` | **7 publications / 29 images** (of 312 / 3,183 released) | NeuroVault publications that are also PubMed **training** papers; the publication and all of its images are dropped |
| `provenance.json` | – | generation time, command, thresholds, counts per reason |

## Provenance

Generated on 2026-10-07 by the full (non-subsampled) run

```bash
cd neurovlm
export HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 HF_DATASETS_OFFLINE=1 TOKENIZERS_PARALLELISM=false
.venv/bin/python scripts/figures/review/core/a4_leakage_audit/run_analysis.py --force --write-exclusions scripts/eval_exclusions/
```

(audit outputs and the full description of every check: `scripts/figures/review/core/a4_leakage_audit/README.md` and
`outputs/`; 40 s with cached MiniLM embeddings, ~13 min cold on the CPU).  Inputs: `neurovlm/neuro_image_papers`
(`publications.parquet`, `coordinates.parquet`), `~/.cache/neurovlm/pmids_split.pt` (23,894 train / 2,987 val / 2,987
test), the released NeuroVault tables of `neurovlm/embedded_text` and the local `~/.cache/neurovlm/neurovault_*.parquet`.

**PubMed** (`pubmed_exclusions.csv`: `pmid, split, reasons, matched_pmid, source, max_score`).  A test- or val-set
publication is listed (by its PMID) when at least one *strict* cross-split check fires against a paper of another split (training paper for 38,
the other held-out split for 3) or a NeuroVault paper (1):

| reason | rule | publications (test + val) |
|---|---|---|
| `d_coordinate_jaccard` | rounded (x, y, z) peak sets with Jaccard ≥ 0.8, ≥ 5 peaks each | 33 |
| `d_identical_coordinate_set` | identical rounded peak set with ≥ 5 peaks | 18 |
| `b_title_fuzzy_vs_train` | normalised title `token_set_ratio` ≥ 95 **and** `token_sort_ratio` ≥ 90, ≥ 25 characters | 9 |
| `c_minilm_abstract_vs_train` | `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` cosine of `title. abstract` ≥ 0.95 | 8 |
| `b_title_fuzzy_vs_neurovault` / `c_minilm_abstract_vs_neurovault` | same rules against the released NeuroVault publications | 1 (val) |

(a publication can carry several reasons; exact DOI sharing across splits never occurs).  `matched_pmid` lists the PMID(s)
(or NeuroVault DOI) it duplicates, `source` where the match lives (`pubmed_train`, `pubmed_val`, `pubmed_test`,
`neurovault_released`).
The hits are reprints / corrections / corrigenda indexed under a second PMID and re-reported peak tables.

**NeuroVault** (`neurovault_exclusions.csv`: `doi, pmid, collection_id, image_ids, n_images, n_images_local_table,
in_released, reasons, matched_train_pmid, score, title_token_set, title_token_sort, minilm_cos`).  Every NeuroVault
publication used in evaluation (released 312 + 21 local-only = 333) is compared with the 23,894 training papers:

| reason | rule | publications |
|---|---|---|
| `f_nv_exact_doi_vs_train` | publication / collection / preprint DOI equals a train publication or coordinate-file DOI | 0 |
| `f_nv_exact_pmid_vs_train` | PMID resolved from the DOI (PubMed tables, all splits) or from PubMed links in the collection metadata is a train PMID | 0 |
| `f_nv_title_vs_train` | normalised title `token_set_ratio` ≥ 95 and strict `token_sort_ratio` ≥ 90 | 7 |
| `f_nv_minilm_vs_train` | MiniLM `title. abstract` cosine ≥ 0.95 | 6 |

All 7 are the same paper as a training PMID whose PubMed row has **no DOI**, which is why the DOI filter of
`02_data/03` let them through.  `doi` is the lower-cased DOI used by `neurovault_text` / `neurovault.pt['df_pubs']` and
by the `doi` column of `neurovault_images_meta` / `neurovault.pt['df_neuro']`; `collection_id` and `image_ids`
(`;`-joined) are NeuroVault ids (`image_ids` = the `id` column of the released image table, i.e. rows of
`neurovault_images`); `pmid` is empty because no NeuroVault paper resolves to a PMID through its DOI;
`matched_train_pmid` is the duplicate training paper.

## How evaluations use it

* `scripts/common.py`: `load_eval_exclusions()` → `{"pubmed_pmids": set[int], "neurovault": DataFrame,
  "neurovault_dois", "neurovault_image_ids", "neurovault_collection_ids", ...}`; `apply_pubmed_exclusions(pmids)` and
  `apply_neurovault_exclusions(dois, image_ids=..., collection_ids=...)` return keep-masks (True = keep) and log the
  number dropped.
* `scripts/validate_models.py` (every PubMed-test and NeuroVault evaluation, the generation sets and the CV folds;
  counts under `meta.exclusions` of `metrics.json` and in `report.md`) and `scripts/figures/main/fig3/4/5/6/S3` (cache
  files carry an `_excl` tag; counts under `eval_exclusions` in `<stem>_text.json`).
* `scripts/figures/review/core/*/`: each isolated sub-directory reads the two CSVs by repo-relative path (its own copy of
  the loader in `_a5_common.py` / `_a6_common.py` / `_review_common.py` or in `run_analysis.py`).
* Every entry point has `--no-exclusions` to reproduce the pre-exclusion numbers for comparison.

Only held-out query items are dropped; training sets are never filtered.  The 5-fold CV of `figures/20` reuses the
same items: its fold 0 held-out part is exactly test ∪ val, so all 42 publications leave fold 0 and folds 1–4 (whose held-out
parts are training-split papers, not covered by the audit's held-out-vs-train checks) are unchanged.

## Regenerate

Re-run the command above after any change to the splits, the PubMed tables or the NeuroVault set; it overwrites the
three files (refused for `--smoke` / `--max-samples` / `--skip-embeddings` runs).  Then re-run the evaluations: the
`_excl` cache tags only protect against caches computed *without* exclusions, so pass `--force` (figures) or a new
`--name` (validation) when the lists themselves change.
