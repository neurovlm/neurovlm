GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090; CPU: AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D 8-Core Processor, 8 threads. int8 = PyTorch dynamic quantisation of the linear layers only, measured after the fp32 model it is made from is released: the token embedding stays fp32 and the output layer, which shares the embedding in bf16, gets its own int8 copy, so the brain-to-text weights take 1.26 GB in int8 against 1.28 GB in bf16. SPECTER2's adapter library wraps most of its linear layers in a subclass that dynamic quantisation skips, so they stay fp32 and text-to-brain int8 runs close to fp32. No phone or embedded device was tested.
